# Orrery
Obsidian-compatible markdown vault shown as a solar system. Claude connects over MCP: reads a small brief at the start of each chat and writes new memories back.

Why: I want AI that remembers what matters to me without burning tokens. Supports [[Goals/This Year]] and reflects [[Core/Values]].
